
# Power BI Dashboard — UX Specification

## Page 1 — Executive Overview

### Header
Sales & Revenue Performance | Date range | Last refresh

### KPI Row
Revenue | Profit | Orders | Units Sold | Profit Margin

### Main Visuals
- Revenue Trend
- Revenue by Region
- Profit by Category
- Top 10 Products

### Filters
Date | Region | Category | Product

## Page 2 — Product & Regional Deep Dive

- Product ranking
- Category contribution
- Regional comparison
- Profitability view
- Customer revenue table

## UX Rules

- Avoid overcrowding.
- Keep KPI definitions consistent.
- Use meaningful titles that answer a question.
- Show comparison where useful.
- Keep filters grouped together.
- Use tooltips for additional detail.
- Use drill-through for deeper product analysis.
